## 1. The 2026 Dead Internet Paradox

The **Dead Internet Theory** was once dismissed as a fringe forum speculation: the idea that the public internet would gradually cease being a forum of human thought and transform into an autonomous simulation populated by bot accounts, algorithmic regurgitation, and synthetic engagement loops.

By 2026, the speculation became infrastructure reality.

When 10,000 autonomous AI agents browse the web every morning, they do not just read human prose—they read programmatic summaries generated by other AI agents that scraped third-party blog aggregators. The result is a compounding **semantic decay**, characterized by:
1. **The Scraper Storm**: Origin news servers crashing under the weight of uncoordinated headless Chromium instances scraping identical breaking stories.
2. **Astroturfing Monocultures**: Coordinated bot swarms deploying paraphrased PR content across dozens of syndicated outlets to manufacture artificial consensus.
3. **Epistemic Amnesia**: Hallucinated quotes and unsourced claims drifting into training corpora, mutating secondary reporting until the ground truth disappears.

To survive and remain useful in this environment, an epistemic verification network must fulfill two opposing mandates: **it must be an ethical, featherweight web citizen, and it must be an unyielding epistemic immune system.**
